Frequently asked questions about the call

How does the call work?

30 minutes on video with Maxens, the founder. You present your business and your project, we look at your current site together if you have one, then we define the right type of site, the pages you need and the timeline.

Is the call really free?

Yes. The call is free and with no commitment. You leave with a clear opinion on your project, whether or not you work with us afterwards.

What should I prepare before the call?

Nothing is required. If you have them, keep at hand the address of your current site, two or three sites you like, and an idea of your budget and launch date.

What happens after the call?

You receive a detailed proposal with the price and the schedule. If it suits you, the project starts with a deposit, and the balance is paid on delivery.

How much does a website or landing page cost?

The price depends on the number of pages, the copy to be written and the features. Packages start at €1,990 and are detailed on the Pricing page.

No time slot works for me, what can I do?

Message us on WhatsApp at +33 7 83 14 12 87 or by email, and we will find a time together.
