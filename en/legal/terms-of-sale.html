Article 1 — Scope

These general terms of sale (the “Terms”) apply, without restriction or reservation, to all services entered into by MSD Media, whatever the client's industry: custom website design, landing page design, website redesign, local search engine optimisation (SEO) and visibility optimisation for generative search engines (GEO).

Most MSD Media clients act as professionals in the course of their business. When a client contracts in a personal capacity, outside any professional activity, they are a consumer within the meaning of the French Consumer Code and therefore benefit from the protective provisions of article 6 bis (Right of withdrawal) of these Terms, in addition to the other provisions of this document.

These Terms are available at any time on the website https://msd-media.com and prevail over any other version or conflicting document. They are systematically sent or handed to each client before the contract is concluded, together with the corresponding quote.

The fact that MSD Media does not rely on any of these Terms at a given time cannot be interpreted as a waiver of its right to rely on them later.

Article 2 — Orders

Each project is the subject of a detailed quote drawn up by MSD Media, specifying the nature of the service, its scope, its price and the indicative delivery time.

The order is only deemed final once the client has accepted the quote in writing (signature, electronic approval or any other equivalent reliable means) and paid the deposit provided for in article 4.

Any change to the initial scope requested by the client after accepting the quote (additional pages, features or content not initially planned) is subject to a supplementary quote and may lead to an adjustment of the delivery time.

Article 3 — Pricing

Services are invoiced at the rates in force on the day the quote is signed, stated in euros. As MSD Media is a micro-enterprise, VAT is not applicable, in accordance with article 293 B of the French General Tax Code — the prices stated are therefore net, with no VAT added.

Indicative prices for the standard packages (landing page, business website) can be found on the Pricing page. Any custom service is subject to a personalised quote drawn up after discussing the client's needs.

Article 4 — Payment terms

Unless otherwise agreed in the quote, payment is made in two stages: a deposit is paid when the project starts, and the balance is due on delivery, after the client has approved the website.

Payment is made by bank transfer or any other means stated on the invoice, within 30 days of the date of issue, unless the parties agree otherwise.

Any late payment automatically incurs penalties calculated on the basis of the interest rate applied by the European Central Bank to its most recent refinancing operation, plus 10 percentage points, as well as a fixed indemnity for recovery costs of €40 (in accordance with articles L.441-10 and D.441-5 of the French Commercial Code).

If the deposit or the balance is not paid, MSD Media reserves the right to suspend the service until the amounts due have actually been paid.

Article 5 — Delivery times

The indicative delivery time is 21 working days from approval of the quote, payment of the deposit and receipt of all the elements needed for the project (content, access, visual identity). This timeline is indicative only and not contractual: it may vary depending on the complexity of the project and how quickly the client provides the expected elements and approvals.

On receiving the delivery, the client has one month to report any non-conformity in writing. After this period, and in the absence of a complaint, the service is deemed compliant and accepted.

Article 6 — Liability and warranty

MSD Media undertakes to perform the services ordered with care and in accordance with professional standards. Its liability can only be engaged in the event of proven fault and is limited exclusively to direct damage suffered by the client, excluding any indirect loss (loss of revenue, customers, reputation or data).

Any complaint relating to non-conformity must be notified to MSD Media in writing within one month of delivery.

In any event, the total amount of compensation that may be owed by MSD Media to the client is strictly limited to the amount actually paid by the client for the service concerned.

Article 6 bis — Right of withdrawal (consumer clients)

In accordance with articles L.221-18 et seq. of the French Consumer Code, a client who has contracted as a consumer (that is, outside any professional activity) has 14 days from the conclusion of the contract to exercise their right of withdrawal, without giving any reason or bearing any costs other than those provided for by law.

To exercise this right, the client notifies MSD Media of their decision by email at maxens.soldan@msd-media.com, before the 14-day period expires.

In accordance with article L.221-28 of the French Consumer Code, a consumer client who wishes the service to begin before the end of the withdrawal period must expressly request it and acknowledges that, in this case, their right of withdrawal ends once MSD Media has fully performed the contract. If the service has only been partially performed at the time of withdrawal, the consumer client remains liable to pay an amount corresponding to the service provided up to the time they communicated their decision to withdraw.

This clause does not apply to clients acting as professionals, for whom only the termination conditions set out in article 13 apply.

If the renegotiation is refused or fails within a reasonable time, the parties may agree to terminate the contract, or jointly ask the court to adapt it. Failing agreement within 6 months, either party may ask the court to terminate the contract.

Article 10 — Specific performance

If either party fails to meet its obligations, the other party may, after formal notice has gone unheeded, seek specific performance of the obligation, unless such performance is impossible or its cost is manifestly unreasonable in relation to its benefit to the creditor.

Article 11 — Exception of non-performance

Each party may suspend the performance of its obligations if the other party does not perform its own, including in advance if a future breach is manifest. The parties agree that reciprocal obligations remain enforceable in principle, unless an impediment lasts more than 30 consecutive days, in which case the provisions of article 12 (Force majeure) apply.

The parties cannot be held liable if the non-performance or delay in performing any of their obligations described in these Terms results from force majeure, within the meaning of article 1218 of the French Civil Code.
