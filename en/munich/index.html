Frequently asked questions: web agency in Munich

Do you work with companies in Munich?

Yes. MSD Media works remotely with companies in France, Switzerland and Belgium, and can support a company based in Germany the same way. We work by video call, in French or English, from brief to delivery.

Do I need to speak French to work with MSD Media?

No. We work in French and English. Sites can be delivered in French, in English or in both languages.

Do you build websites in German?

We do not write German copy. For a German version, we integrate the copy provided by your translator, with the same layout and the same technical SEO.

How long does it take to build a website?

A first version is delivered in 7 days and the final version in 21 days, in 4 steps: brief and kick-off, design and copy, development, delivery and adjustments.

How much does a website or landing page cost?

The price depends on the number of pages, the copy to write, the languages and the features. Every offer is detailed on our pricing page, and a 30-minute call is enough to get a precise quote.

Can you help me rank on Google in France?

Yes. Every site ships with technical SEO in place: fast pages, tags, structured data and a sitemap. Rankings then take from a few weeks to a few months depending on competition, with no promise of position.

Who is behind MSD Media?

MSD Media was founded in June 2025 in Annecy by Maxens Soldan, a member of the French Tech Munich community. He is your single point of contact, from the first call to launch.

Do I own the website once it is delivered?

Yes. The source code, the domain name and all access are handed over as soon as the balance is paid. Any developer can take over the site.
