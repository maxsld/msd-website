FAQ

Which MSD Care level should I choose?

Essential suits freelancers and landing pages: the site stays online, backed up and up to date, with 30 minutes of changes per month. Growth is for clinics, practices and showcase websites that want to grow on Google and in AI answers, with 2 SEO pieces per month. Brand is designed for brands and laboratories, with priority changes, a compliance review and a monthly call.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Yes, 3 months minimum, whatever the level. After that, MSD Care continues month to month, and either party can end it by email with one month's notice.

Can I change level?

Yes, at the end of each month, on simple written request.

What happens if I don't use my time for changes?

Time for changes not used in a month is not carried over to the next month.

What does the Brand compliance review cover?

Every month, a review of the website content against healthcare and medical device communication rules, the legal notice, the cookie banner and accessibility.

Is MSD Care mandatory?

No. The website belongs to you, and a custom-coded site needs little upkeep. MSD Care is an option for those who would rather hand everything over after launch.
