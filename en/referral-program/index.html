Referral FAQ

How does the MSD Media referral program work?

You introduce us to a company with a web or digital need. If the project is signed, you earn a 15% commission on the signed amount.

Who can recommend a contact?

Freelancers, agencies, consultants, business owners, clients or friends can recommend an opportunity. No particular legal status is required to join the program: the terms adapt to your situation, as long as the contact is qualified and genuinely interested.

Which types of projects qualify?

Recommendations can cover a new website, a redesign, a landing page, branding, SEO, copywriting or a more specific web project.

Which contacts are you really looking for?

Any company with a real need for a website or landing page, whatever its industry. MSD Media performs particularly well with law firms, healthcare professionals, real estate agencies, restaurants, tradespeople, architects, coaches, consultants and startups. If in doubt, introduce the contact anyway: we will assess it together.

How much is the commission?

The commission is 15% of the amount signed with the recommended client. It is calculated on the approved project, excluding tax.

When is the commission paid?

The commission is paid by bank transfer as soon as the project starts and the client's first payment is confirmed.

Is the commission due if the contact does not sign?

No. The commission is only triggered if the contact you introduced becomes a client and signs a project with MSD Media.

How do I send a recommendation?

Fill in the form at the bottom of the page with your details, the recommended company, the estimated budget and the context of the need.

What happens after I send the form?

We review the context, contact the company if the opportunity is relevant, then keep you informed of progress if the project goes ahead.

Do I have to handle the sale or client follow-up?

No. Your role is to make the introduction or pass on the contact. MSD Media then takes over the qualification, the quote and the sale.

Will my contacts know that I earn a commission?

Not from MSD Media: this information is never shared without your explicit consent. You are free to be transparent with them or not — most referrers prefer to stay discreet, which is entirely possible.

Do I need to sign a contract or commit to a volume?

No volume commitment, no exclusivity, no binding contract. You introduce contacts whenever you like, at your own pace. A simple business introducer agreement formalises the relationship when the first commission is paid.

Is the program free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no joining fee and no minimum number of recommendations.

Can I recommend several companies?

Yes. You can send several recommendations, as long as they are serious, qualified and consistent with MSD Media's services.

What happens if several people recommend the same contact?

Attribution is assessed case by case, primarily based on the first qualified recommendation received and the strength of the relationship with the contact.
