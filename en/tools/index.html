Frequently asked questions about the tools

Are these tools really free?

Yes. They are free, with no sign-up and no email address required. They show you concretely what a well-built website changes for your visibility.

Do the tools store the sites analysed?

No. We read the public page at the time of the analysis, as a search engine would, and we keep neither the result nor the list of sites analysed.

Do these tools replace a full audit?

No. They analyse one page at a time and spot the most common problems. A full audit looks at the whole site, the competition and your goals: Maxens can do it with you.
