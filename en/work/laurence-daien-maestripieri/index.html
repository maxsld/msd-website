Laurence Daien Maestripieri

Strong expertise without a digital presence

Laurence Daien already had solid credibility. Her experience, the quality of her references and the finesse of her coaching were very real. Yet all of this was hardly visible online. This is common among experts who have long relied on word of mouth: the reputation exists, but it does not yet have a digital presence to match.

The project's goal was therefore ambitious in the right way. It was not just about creating a business website. It was about building a web presence able to extend an established reputation without betraying it. For a premium public speaking coach and trainer, the site had to convey trust, elegance, precision and real attentiveness. All with enough clarity to encourage people to get in touch.

Laurence coaches executives, managers and experts who need to speak with impact, embody their message and become more precise when speaking. This professional reality had to be visible on the site straight away, authentically, without over-promising.

Positioning challenge

The first challenge was to find the right level of premium. A site that was too cold would have weakened the human side of the coaching. A site that was too showy would have felt artificial. A subtler balance was needed: premium without arrogance, professional without rigidity, personal without amateurism.

The second challenge was how to tell the story of the expertise. When a professional has a rich background and great references, it is tempting to pile up proof and authority arguments. Yet that is not the best approach. The message had to be organised so that visitors first understand the essentials, then gradually discover the depth of the profile.

The website's strategic intent

The site had to fulfil three functions at once. First, immediately establish the credibility of Laurence's personal brand. Next, let a prospect quickly understand the nature of public speaking coaching. Finally, support more consistent SEO around her activity and her local presence in Annecy.

So we designed the project as a positioning website, not as a simple online business card. Each section had to help build a perception. The design, the copy, the place of references, the reading rhythm and the calls to action all had to point in the same direction. When this kind of consistency is there, the website becomes a natural extension of the personal brand.

Work on the copywriting

The copywriting was handled with great care, as it was at the heart of the project. A public speaking coach's website cannot afford weak or generic copy. The quality of the language directly shapes how the professional is perceived. The wording therefore had to be more accurate, more embodied and more aligned with the finesse of the positioning.

We aimed to bring out the value of the coaching better, without falling into empty slogans. The messages were structured to guide the reader, clarify the proposition and gradually build trust. The text had to stay readable, breathe, and leave room for Laurence's own voice. This kind of work is what turns a decent website into a truly consistent one.

Design, rhythm and readability

The art direction was deliberately clean. The goal was not to overload the interface, but to let the message breathe and convey a sense of control. On a project like this, minimalism is not a gratuitous aesthetic effect. It helps support the premium level and gives the message room.

The user journey was also carefully structured. A good business website must allow quick reading while offering enough depth to those who want to go further. Laurence's site was therefore designed as a smooth journey, where each block prepares the next. This progression is essential to avoid breaking the trust that builds as visitors move forward.

Local SEO and editorial relevance

SEO was not treated as an artificial layer added afterwards. It was built into the editorial structure from the start. The content was organised around public speaking, coaching, training and her base in Annecy. The goal was to give Google a clearer reading of the positioning while keeping a premium tone.

This approach is often more effective than simply repeating keywords. When pages genuinely talk about the profession, the offer and the area in a consistent way, SEO becomes stronger. It relies on the quality of the page, not on tricks. That is exactly what we aimed for here.

Performance and SEO optimisation

In November 2026, we reworked the site in depth without touching the design or the copy. Every image was converted to WebP and resized to its display size, the testimonial videos were compressed and now only load on click, and the gallery only loads as the visitor scrolls towards it. The result: the home page went from around 10 MB to just over 1 MB, and the mobile PageSpeed score from 61 to 95.

On the SEO side, every page received a title and description built for local search in Annecy, a canonical URL, complete Open Graph tags and Schema.org structured data (local business, services, FAQ, breadcrumbs). The site was also migrated to Vercel over HTTPS, with clean redirects and optimised caching.

Results

  • #1 on Google for her main query from indexing — local SEO in Annecy
  • PageSpeed score: 95/100 mobile, 100/100 desktop
  • Display time: < 1 s on desktop
  • Page weight cut by 10: WebP images, compressed videos, lazy loading
  • Technical SEO: 100/100, complete structured data (local business, services, FAQ)
  • Delivery: 10 working days
  • Zero WordPress: lightweight native code, no CMS vulnerabilities

What this website brings

The site finally gives Laurence a digital presence that matches her level of expertise. It does not replace word of mouth, it amplifies it. It confirms an impression of quality, frames the relationship better from the first contact and supports a more complete image online.

Above all, this case study shows that a premium website is not just about design. It is about consistency between the person, the message, the structure and the final perception. That consistency is what lets a business website truly work for a personal brand.
