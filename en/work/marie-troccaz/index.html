Marie Troccaz

MSD Media created the business website of Marie Troccaz, a consultant in corporate culture, team cohesion and human leadership: built from scratch, with copywriting and integrated SEO.

Strong expertise, without a digital presence to match

Marie Troccaz supports companies on demanding topics: corporate culture, team cohesion and human leadership. Her expertise was clear, and so was her vision, but she had no website to carry that level of value. It is a classic case among independent experts who do good work: the substance is there, so is the credibility, but there is no digital presence able to convey it properly.

The project therefore started from a blank page. There was nothing to refresh, nothing to fix visually, nothing to update. A complete, consistent, 100% custom presence had to be built. That changes the nature of the work. When everything has to be created, every choice matters more: the vocabulary, the structure, the order of the arguments, how the offer is presented, how the person is introduced, and the overall impression in the first seconds.

A goal that looks very simple

The goal was not just for Marie to be online. That ambition is too weak for a website that must really serve the business. The real goal was much more precise: within two seconds, people should understand who she is, what she brings to leaders and teams, and why her coaching makes the difference.

That level of clarity requires real groundwork. A page that tries to say everything often ends up making nothing clear. Conversely, a good business website selects the right messages, puts them in the right order and conveys the positioning without diluting it. For Marie, this was essential, because her work relies precisely on a quality human relationship, a clear stance and a coaching promise that must feel credible straight away.

The blank page challenge

Starting from scratch is always both an advantage and a constraint. The advantage is that you can build a clean system, with no visual debt or shaky messaging to fix. The constraint is that everything has to be right from the start. So we had to lay a solid foundation for Marie's personal brand, without falling into the clichés of coaching websites that are too predictable, too vague or too interchangeable.

We tried to avoid two common mistakes. The first would be a site that is too corporate, losing the human side of her coaching. The second would be a site that is too soft or abstract, failing to convey the rigour and precision of her work. The right balance had to combine clarity, depth, trust and professionalism.

Work on positioning and messaging

The editorial structure was designed as a framing tool. Visitors first had to understand who Marie is, then the world she works in, and finally why her approach is different. We organised the message to highlight her specialisation in corporate culture, cohesion and human leadership, without drowning the reading in overly theoretical wording.

Copywriting played a central role. A site like this cannot settle for clean text. It must express a stance. The words had to carry a level of maturity, clarity and subtlety consistent with the quality of the coaching. We aimed for a simple, accurate, human tone that is never vague. That is what lets visitors feel they are dealing with a real professional, not surface-level talk.

A page designed for leaders and teams

One of the project's difficulties was speaking to decision-makers and to the reality of teams at the same time. Marie does not sell an abstract idea of leadership. She works on very concrete human dynamics. The site therefore had to show that her coaching addresses real issues: cohesion, management, culture, communication, alignment, stance.

This led us to build a page that talks about tangible benefits without making easy promises. Visitors had to recognise themselves quickly in the problems addressed. When that recognition happens, conversion becomes more natural. The site no longer needs to overdo it. It just needs to be accurate, well structured and convincing.

Design, structure and a 2-second read

The design was treated as an extension of the positioning. Since the goal was for people to understand very quickly who she is and what she brings, the page had to be readable immediately. So we favoured a clear structure, a simple but strong hero, a clean hierarchy and a reading rhythm without overload.

The site was not meant to impress with effects. It had to convince through accuracy. This is an important point on premium personal brand projects: design must support the message, not compete with it. Here, visual clarity directly serves commercial clarity. Visitors can scan the page quickly, identify the core of the positioning and get in touch without unnecessary friction.

SEO angle and semantic consistency

The SEO work started from the real core of the profession. Rather than stuffing the page with artificial phrases, we strengthened the signals around corporate coaching, corporate culture, cohesion and human leadership. This angle links the page more clearly to Marie's world while keeping a very natural reading level.

This semantic consistency matters for two reasons. First, it helps Google understand the nature of the service. Second, it strengthens visitors' immediate understanding. When a page names things well, it gains in both SEO and conversion. That is always the aim on a well-built business website.

What this website changes

The new site gives Marie a digital presence that matches her expertise. It makes her offer more visible, clearer and more credible. It lets her present herself with much more strength and consistency, without betraying the subtlety of her world.

This project shows that a well-used blank page is a real opportunity. When everything is built to measure, the message, design and conversion can be aligned from day one. For Marie, the site is not just about being online. It is about making people understand, very quickly, what gives her coaching its value. That was the real goal, and it guided the whole project.
