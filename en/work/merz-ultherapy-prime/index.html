Merz Aesthetics — Ultherapy® Prime

MSD Media designed the dedicated Ultherapy® Prime landing page for Merz Aesthetics: clinical credibility, a clear patient experience and a structure geared towards finding a practitioner.

Context of a medical product launch

Merz Aesthetics is a leading international aesthetic medicine laboratory. For the launch of Ultherapy® Prime, a micro-focused ultrasound (HIFU) technology for skin tightening on the face, neck and body, the brand needed a dedicated landing page designed for the French market, able to carry an international message while speaking directly to local patients and practitioners.

For an aesthetic medicine product, the page does not just present an offer: it must establish immediate clinical credibility, reassure a demanding audience and guide users towards a concrete action, finding a practitioner authorised to offer the treatment.

Problems to solve

The first challenge was to turn a scientific, international message (technology, protocols, clinical studies) into an accessible reading journey, without losing the rigour expected for a medical device. Too much technicality pushes patients away; too much simplification weakens credibility with practitioners.

The second challenge was conversion. A pharmaceutical brand does not sell the treatment directly: it must direct users to a partner practitioner. The page therefore had to be structured to lead naturally to that step, without breaking the reading experience.

Goal of the landing page

The goal was to create a page that meets both requirements at once: a product showcase desirable and clear enough for the general public, and a resource serious enough to meet the communication standards of an aesthetic medicine laboratory.

We built the page as a progressive journey, from patient benefit to clinical proof, then to the practitioner search, keeping the premium and reassuring tone expected for this kind of product at every step.

Work on the structure and journey

The page was organised into modular blocks: presentation of the benefit (tightening, non-invasive, no downtime), explanation of the protocol, the distinction between face and body uses, then social and clinical proof. Each section answers a question visitors naturally ask before moving on to the next step.

The practitioner locator was built in as the natural end point of the journey rather than as an isolated link, so that conversion follows on from reading instead of feeling like a sales interruption.

Design and brand credibility

The design had to carry Merz Aesthetics' visual codes while staying readable and calming. For a medical product aimed at older and younger audiences alike, the balance between premium aesthetics and functional clarity is decisive: the page must be appealing without ever seeming superficial on a health topic.

Before/after comparisons and testimonials were included with a restrained presentation, to support the proof without slipping into an overly promotional register, which is hard to reconcile with medical device communication.

SEO and semantic structure

The SEO work focused on the product's semantic structure: the name of the technology, treated areas, benefits and FAQ were organised to clearly answer patients' informational queries in France, while keeping the terminology approved by the brand for this type of device.

The FAQ was designed as an entry point for specific searches (duration, results, pain, indications), so that the page answers the concrete questions that usually come before the decision to consult a practitioner.

Results

  • Delivery: 18 working days — a timeline set by a fixed-date international product launch
  • No editorial gap between the international scientific message and the French-speaking patient reading

Deliverables

  • Dedicated product landing page
  • Structured patient journey
  • Integrated practitioner locator
  • Design aligned with the Merz Aesthetics brand guidelines
  • FAQ and SEO semantic structure
  • Presentation of clinical proof and before/after results

What the landing page brings

The page gives Ultherapy® Prime a clear and consistent presence on the French market, able to carry both the scientific rigour expected from an aesthetic medicine laboratory and the accessibility needed to guide a patient towards a decision.

This case study illustrates how a product landing page, even within a strict medical framework, can remain a real conversion lever when structure, design and wording all work in the same direction.

What the journey makes measurable

To evaluate this kind of page, the useful indicators are reading of the product information, access to the practitioner search and the actions taken in that tool. They separate interest in the content from moving to the next step. This study presents our design choices; it does not publish the laboratory's traffic or conversion results.
